I’m Yashar. I work in engineering, mostly around SRE, platform engineering, and engineering management.

I started this blog mostly as a place to write things down for my future self. Over time, it turned into a place where I write about engineering, management, careers, reliability, and lessons I pick up along the way.

Some posts come from things I’ve experienced at work, some from things I’ve read or learned, and some are simply thoughts I wanted to think through by writing them down.

I don’t really have a grand plan for this blog. I write about the things I find interesting — and hopefully, some of them are useful to someone else too. :)
